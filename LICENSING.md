# Licensing and attribution

## Theme source

The Nebula theme source has no project-wide license. No MIT or other blanket license applies to the whole project.

For permission to redistribute or relicense the theme source, contact the maintainer. A bundled font license does not license the surrounding theme.

## Bundled fonts

The font binaries are unmodified and distributed under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1**. Preserve the corresponding notices when redistributing them.

| Font | License | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sora | [OFL 1.1](fonts/LICENSE-Sora.txt) | [Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/sora) |
| Oxanium | [OFL 1.1](fonts/LICENSE-Oxanium.txt) | [Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/oxanium) |
| JetBrains Mono | [OFL 1.1](fonts/LICENSE-JetBrains-Mono.txt) | [Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/jetbrainsmono) |

Exact font URLs and checksums are retained in `fonts/ttf-sources.json` and `fonts/woff2-sources.json`.
